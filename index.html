How a project ships.

  1. 00

    Prototype

    In week one you hold something playable — rough, but real enough to argue about.

  2. 01

    Playtest

    Real people try it every week. We keep what they love and cut what they skip.

  3. 10

    Polish

    Motion, sound, haptics, every text size, both light and dark. The part people feel.

  4. 11

    Ship

    App Store, Google Play, Steam or the web — then updates, because launch is day one.
