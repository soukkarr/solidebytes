Contact support

Email us and a real person from the studio will reply within two working days.

hello@solidebytes.com

Please tell us the app name, your device (for example iPhone 16 or Pixel 9), its system version, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Restore purchases

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose the app and tap Cancel Subscription.

Android: open Google Play subscriptions, choose the app and tap Cancel subscription.

Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds are requested from them:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help

If something you bought didn’t arrive or doesn’t work, email us too and we’ll sort it out.

Delete your account

In any app with accounts, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and its data are permanently deleted within 30 days.

If you no longer have the app, email hello@solidebytes.com from the address linked to your account, tell us which app, and we’ll delete it for you. Purchase records we must keep for tax are kept as described in our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription; please cancel it first.

Report a bug or suggest an idea

Email hello@solidebytes.com with the app name, your device and system version, and the steps that cause the problem. Ideas are just as welcome; many of our features started as an email.

Privacy requests

To see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, email hello@solidebytes.com. We reply within one month. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
