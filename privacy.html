Who we are

SolideBytes Ltd (“SolideBytes”, “we”, “us”) designs and publishes apps and games. We are a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 17533878, with our registered office at 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom.

We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. This policy covers our website and every app and game we publish under the SolideBytes name on the Apple App Store, Google Play and the web. If an app has its own privacy notice, that notice adds to this one.

Our website

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics and does not load trackers or third-party fonts. There are no forms.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record your IP address in server logs to keep the service secure. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and we use it only to reply.

Our apps and games

Most of our apps work without collecting any personal data at all. What an app uses depends on its features. Each app’s privacy label on the App Store and its Data safety section on Google Play list exactly what it collects, and the table below names each app.

Data stored on your device or in your iCloud

Things you create, such as notes, save games and settings, stay on your device or in your own iCloud or Google account. We can’t see them.

Accounts

If an app lets you sign in, for example with Sign in with Apple, Google or Game Center, we receive the identifier and the details you choose to share, such as your name or email address. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your real email address. We use this only to run your account, sync your progress and support you. You can delete your account at any time; see Delete your account.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple or Google process all payments. We receive confirmation of what you bought (product, date, country and a transaction ID) so we can unlock it and restore it on your other devices. We never receive your name, card or billing details from them.

Analytics and crash reports

Some apps send anonymous or pseudonymous diagnostics, such as device model, system version, app version, crash logs and which screens or levels are used, so we can fix bugs and improve the app. We do not use this data to identify you or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Advertising

Some free games show ads. On iPhone and iPad, ad partners can use your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow it when the app asks under App Tracking Transparency. If you don’t, you’ll still see ads, just not personalised ones. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Permissions

An app asks for access to things like your camera, photos, location or notifications only when a feature needs it, and it still works when you say no.

Why we use your data

Under UK and EU data protection law (the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the EU GDPR), we rely on these legal bases:

PurposeLegal basis
Running your account, delivering purchases, syncing progressPerformance of a contract
Answering your emails and support requestsLegitimate interests (helping you)
Crash reports and anonymous diagnosticsLegitimate interests (keeping our apps working and secure)
Personalised ads and trackingConsent, which you can withdraw at any time
Keeping purchase and tax recordsLegal obligation

Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that host, analyse or support our apps on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions. Each app’s providers are named in the table above.
  • Apple and Google, as the app stores that process payments and distribute our apps, under their own privacy policies.
  • Ad partners, only in apps that show ads, and only as described under Advertising.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our users’ safety or our legal rights.

International transfers

Some service providers are outside the UK and the European Economic Area. When data leaves the UK or EEA, we make sure it is protected by an adequacy decision or by safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after your last message.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: 6 years, as UK tax law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy to take elsewhere, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@solidebytes.com and we’ll reply within one month. It’s free.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or to the data protection authority in your EU country.

California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don’t sell personal information. To opt out of personalised ads, decline tracking when an app asks, or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and providers with strong security records. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
